# v

`/v` is `/validate`, typed in one character because it is the checkpoint worth
reaching for often and a checkpoint nobody reaches for is not one.

Invoke the `validate` skill and follow it exactly - the three-part reply, the
going and checking that the middle word demands, and the stop at the end. Any
argument given to `/v` is the argument to `validate`.

Nothing here changes, adds to or shortens what that skill says. This file exists
to make the keystroke short, and where it ever reads as disagreeing with
`validate`, `validate` is right.
