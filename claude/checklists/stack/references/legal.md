# Legal surface

Small pieces of work, large absences.

Privacy, terms and contact are served, and all three are reachable **from the site's
own footer** — not the agency bar, which carries no client links. A page that exists
and is linked from nowhere is a page nobody finds when they go looking, which is the
only time anybody looks.

A site collecting an email address without a privacy page is collecting it without
saying what happens to it.

Write them for the business rather than pasting a template; a privacy notice describing
data collection the site does not do is worse than none.

## Consent

A cookie notice belongs where a tracker is, and nowhere else. A consent banner on a
site that sets no cookies is theatre and costs every visitor a click. Where one is
needed, it defaults to the most privacy-preserving option.

The footer's own links — the paths, whether they resolve — are checked here. How the
footer looks is `/design-checklist`.
