### MM3 · Grouped columns
Two to four columns, each under a small caps heading that names a category. The
headings are the design: a visitor reads four words and knows which column is
theirs before reading a single link.

*Use when:* more than eight destinations that fall into real categories.
*Don't confuse with:* MM1, which is one ungrouped column.

```html
<div class="panel panel--groups">
  <section><h3>By service</h3><ul>…</ul></section>
  <section><h3>By industry</h3><ul>…</ul></section>
</div>
```

*Anti-pattern:* a category with one item under it. Either it belongs in another
column or the grouping is invented.
