### MM4 · Full-bleed canvas
The panel spans the viewport rather than sitting under its trigger, with the
content set on a wide grid and generous vertical air. The width is the statement:
it turns a menu into a page-sized moment.

*Use when:* a large catalogue, or a brand that can carry the theatre of it.
*Don't confuse with:* MM3, which is columns inside a bounded card.

```html
<div class="panel panel--bleed">
  <div class="container panel__grid">…</div>
</div>
```

*Anti-pattern:* full-bleed width with six links in it. The empty canvas reads as
a mistake rather than confidence.
