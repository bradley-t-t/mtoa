# Whose setup this is

The rules in this file are mine alone and sit on top of the shared ones in
`~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`, which imports it. Everything that names me, my machines or
my accounts goes in `mtoa.json` beside it; what goes here is how I want to be
worked with that nobody else would want copied.

Some examples of what belongs here:

- Who else works with me, and what a session may and may not send them.
- Which accounts are read-only to an agent unless I approve the specific write.
- Where records live that the tools do not hold.
